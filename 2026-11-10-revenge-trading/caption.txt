Got stopped out on gold? Save this for the next time it happens, because it'll happen.

You lose $30, you're annoyed, and you jump straight back in with a bigger lot and no plan because you just want it back. That's revenge trading. And it's how a $30 loss turns into $90.

So here's what I'd do in gold trading after a stop-out:
1. Walk away for 30 minutes. Chart closed.
2. Set a daily loss limit before I start, and when I hit it I'm done for the day.
3. Write the loss in my journal, what happened and how I felt.

Boring? Yep. It works.

Education only, not financial advice. Trading carries risk.
